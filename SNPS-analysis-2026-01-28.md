## Investment Thesis Summary

Synopsys is a dominant player in a near-duopoly EDA market with 38% market share, benefiting from secular tailwinds in AI chip design and semiconductor complexity. The completed $35B Ansys acquisition creates a unique "silicon-to-systems" design stack. Current valuation is at multi-year lows relative to historical averages, though profitability metrics have temporarily weakened due to acquisition integration. For a moderate-risk investor with a 1-5 year horizon, SNPS offers a quality business at a more reasonable valuation entry point.

## Company Overview

**Business Description**: Synopsys provides electronic design automation (EDA) software and semiconductor intellectual property (IP) used to design and verify integrated circuits. Founded in 1986, headquartered in Sunnyvale, CA.

**Revenue Segments**:
- Design Automation (~65%): EDA tools for digital/analog chip design
- Design IP (~25%): Pre-verified silicon IP blocks
- Application Security (~10%): Being divested

**Key Products**:
- Synopsys.ai (AI-driven EDA suite)
- Fusion Compiler, VCS, PrimeTime, TestMAX
- Interface IP, Memory IP, Security IP

**Geographic Revenue**: US (45%), China (16%), Rest of World (39%)

**Recent Strategic Moves**:
- Completed Ansys acquisition ($35B) - July 2025
- NVIDIA $2B strategic investment at $414.79/share
- Selling Processor IP business to GlobalFoundries
- 10% workforce restructuring ($300-350M charges)
