# Destination Research Guide

Detailed strategies for researching destinations based on trip type and traveler preferences.

## Research Strategies by Trip Type

### Beach/Resort Vacation

**Key Search Queries**
- `best beaches in [destination] [month]`
- `[destination] beach resort reviews`
- `[destination] water temperature [month]`
- `[destination] rainy season`

**What to Research**
| Factor | Why It Matters | What to Look For |
|--------|---------------|------------------|
| Weather patterns | Beach enjoyment | Avg temp, rainfall, humidity |
| Water conditions | Swimming/snorkeling | Temperature, visibility, currents |
| Beach type | Preference matching | Sand type, crowd level, amenities |
| Resort amenities | Value assessment | Pools, restaurants, activities |
| All-inclusive value | Budget planning | What's included vs. extra |

**Red Flags**
- Seaweed season (Caribbean: Mar-Oct peak)
- Monsoon/rainy seasons
- Jellyfish seasons
- Construction during off-peak times

### City/Cultural Trip

**Key Search Queries**
- `[city] neighborhoods guide`
- `[city] local vs tourist areas`
- `[city] museum pass worth it`
- `free things to do in [city]`
- `[city] day trips`

**What to Research**
| Factor | Why It Matters | What to Look For |
|--------|---------------|------------------|
| Neighborhood character | Where to stay | Vibe, safety, transit access |
| Museum/attraction passes | Cost savings | City passes, combo tickets |
| Opening hours | Itinerary planning | Closure days, late nights |
| Reservation requirements | Avoid disappointment | Popular sites booking windows |
| Local customs | Cultural respect | Dress codes, tipping, etiquette |

**Neighborhood Selection Criteria**
- Proximity to main attractions
- Public transit access
- Restaurant/nightlife options
- Safety ratings
- Authentic vs. tourist atmosphere
- Budget alignment

### Adventure/Outdoor Trip

**Key Search Queries**
- `[activity] in [destination] best time of year`
- `[destination] [activity] tour operators reviews`
- `[destination] hiking trails difficulty`
- `[activity] gear rental [destination]`
- `[destination] outdoor permits required`

**What to Research**
| Factor | Why It Matters | What to Look For |
|--------|---------------|------------------|
| Season/conditions | Safety and experience | Weather windows, trail conditions |
| Permit requirements | Legal compliance | Booking windows, costs |
| Guide requirements | Safety/regulations | Mandatory guides, recommended |
| Fitness level needed | Proper preparation | Altitude, distance, technical skill |
| Gear availability | Packing decisions | Rental quality, costs |

**Safety Considerations**
- Altitude acclimatization needs
- Wildlife encounters
- Weather pattern changes
- Emergency evacuation options
- Travel insurance coverage for activities

### Family Trip (with Children)

**Key Search Queries**
- `[destination] with kids [age range]`
- `family-friendly hotels [destination]`
- `[destination] stroller friendly`
- `kid-friendly restaurants [destination]`
- `[destination] playgrounds parks`

**What to Research**
| Factor | Why It Matters | What to Look For |
|--------|---------------|------------------|
| Kid-friendly attractions | Engagement | Age-appropriate activities |
| Accommodation amenities | Convenience | Cribs, pools, kitchens |
| Restaurant options | Feeding logistics | Kids menus, high chairs |
| Stroller accessibility | Mobility | Cobblestones, stairs, transit |
| Nap/rest opportunities | Schedule management | Hotel proximity, quiet spaces |

**Age-Specific Considerations**

| Age Group | Key Needs | Activity Duration | Pace |
|-----------|-----------|-------------------|------|
| Infants (0-1) | Feeding, naps, changing | Short | Very slow |
| Toddlers (1-3) | Naps, snacks, movement | 1-2 hours max | Slow |
| Preschool (3-5) | Engagement, snacks, rest | 2-3 hours | Moderate |
| School age (6-12) | Education, fun, activity | 3-4 hours | Moderate |
| Teens (13+) | Independence, interests | Full day | Normal |

## Seasonal Considerations

### Northern Hemisphere Seasons

| Season | Months | Best For | Considerations |
|--------|--------|----------|----------------|
| Spring | Mar-May | Europe, Japan (cherry blossoms), US cities | Shoulder season prices, variable weather |
| Summer | Jun-Aug | Beach destinations, Nordic countries | Peak prices, crowds, heat |
| Fall | Sep-Nov | Wine regions, foliage destinations | Pleasant weather, fewer crowds |
| Winter | Dec-Feb | Ski resorts, southern escapes, Christmas markets | Holidays expensive, weather dependent |

### Southern Hemisphere Seasons

| Season | Months | Best For | Considerations |
|--------|--------|----------|----------------|
| Summer | Dec-Feb | Australia, New Zealand, South America beaches | Christmas/NY peak pricing |
| Fall | Mar-May | Patagonia, South Africa | Shoulder season |
| Winter | Jun-Aug | Ski in Andes, wildlife in Africa | Dry season in many regions |
| Spring | Sep-Nov | Wildflowers, jacarandas | Shoulder season |

### Tropical Destinations

| Pattern | Months | What to Expect |
|---------|--------|----------------|
| Dry season | Varies by location | Higher prices, better weather |
| Wet/Green season | Varies by location | Lower prices, afternoon showers, lush scenery |
| Hurricane season | Jun-Nov (Atlantic) | Travel insurance essential, deals available |

## Destination Categories by Budget

### Budget-Friendly Destinations
- Southeast Asia (Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia)
- Eastern Europe (Portugal, Czech Republic, Hungary)
- Central America (Guatemala, Nicaragua)
- India
- Mexico (outside resort areas)

**Budget Strategies**
- Travel in shoulder seasons
- Stay in neighborhoods just outside center
- Use public transit
- Eat where locals eat
- Free walking tours
- Museum free days

### Mid-Range Destinations
- Western Europe (off-peak)
- Japan (with planning)
- Australia/New Zealand
- Costa Rica
- South Africa

### Luxury Destinations
- Maldives
- Switzerland
- French Polynesia
- Scandinavia
- Luxury Safari destinations

## Accommodation Selection Matrix

| Accommodation Type | Best For | Pros | Cons |
|-------------------|----------|------|------|
| **Luxury Hotel** | Special occasions, business | Service, amenities, location | Cost, impersonal |
| **Boutique Hotel** | Couples, unique experience | Character, personalized | Limited amenities |
| **Chain Hotel** | Reliability, points | Consistency, loyalty perks | Generic |
| **Vacation Rental** | Families, long stays | Space, kitchen, local feel | No daily service |
| **Hostel** | Budget, social | Cost, meeting people | Shared spaces, noise |
| **Resort** | Relaxation, all-in-one | Convenience, activities | Can feel isolated |
| **B&B/Guesthouse** | Character, local tips | Personal touch, breakfast | Less privacy |

## Research Verification

Always cross-reference information from multiple sources:

1. **Official tourism sites** - Accurate but promotional
2. **Recent blog posts** (within 1 year) - Practical tips
3. **Review sites** (TripAdvisor, Google) - Current conditions
4. **Reddit/forums** - Honest opinions, recent experiences
5. **News sources** - Safety, closures, events

**Red Flags in Research**
- Information over 2 years old
- Only positive reviews
- Prices that seem too good
- Vague or missing contact info
- No recent photos
