## Transportation Cost Planning

### Flights

**Booking Timing**
| Trip Type | Optimal Booking Window |
|-----------|----------------------|
| Domestic | 1-3 months ahead |
| International | 2-6 months ahead |
| Peak season | 4-6 months ahead |
| Last minute | Check 2-3 weeks out for deals |

**Cost Reduction Strategies**
- Flexible dates (±3 days can save 20-40%)
- Alternative airports
- One-stop vs. direct tradeoff
- Tuesday/Wednesday departures often cheaper
- Incognito browsing for searches
- Set price alerts
- Consider positioning flights for cheaper hubs

**Hidden Costs to Include**
- Seat selection fees
- Baggage fees (checked and carry-on for budget airlines)
- In-flight meals
- Airport transfers
- Lounge access (if desired)

### Ground Transportation

| Mode | Best For | Typical Cost |
|------|----------|--------------|
| **Rental Car** | Rural areas, road trips, families | $30-100/day + fuel + parking |
| **Public Transit** | Cities, budget travel | $2-15/day |
| **Rideshare** | Short trips, airport | $10-50/ride |
| **Private Transfer** | Airport, groups | $50-150/ride |
| **Train** | Europe, scenic routes | Varies widely |

**Rental Car Hidden Costs**
- Insurance (CDW/LDW): $15-30/day
- GPS: $10-15/day (use phone instead)
- Additional drivers: $10-15/day
- Young driver fee: $20-30/day
- One-way drop-off: $50-200+
- Fuel policy surcharges
- Parking: $0-50/day
- Tolls

| Type | Budget | Mid-Range | Luxury |
|------|--------|-----------|--------|
| **Hostel** | $15-40 | N/A | N/A |
| **Budget Hotel** | $50-100 | $100-150 | N/A |
| **Mid-Range Hotel** | N/A | $150-250 | $250-350 |
| **Luxury Hotel** | N/A | N/A | $350-1000+ |
| **Vacation Rental** | $75-150 | $150-300 | $300-1000+ |
| **Resort** | $100-200 | $250-500 | $500-2000+ |

**Total Accommodation Cost Formula**
```
Base rate × nights + taxes (10-20%) + resort fees ($20-50/night) + parking
```

**Savings Strategies**
- Book direct for perks (breakfast, upgrades)
- Use loyalty programs
- Check package deals (flight + hotel)
- Consider location tradeoffs (center vs. transit-accessible)
- Longer stays often have weekly discounts
- Non-refundable rates save 10-20%

## Logistics Planning

### Timeline for Trip Preparation

| Timeframe | Tasks |
|-----------|-------|
| **3-6 months out** | Book flights, apply for visas, book popular accommodations |
| **2-3 months out** | Book accommodations, reserve must-do activities |
| **1 month out** | Book restaurants, finalize itinerary, arrange transport |
| **2 weeks out** | Confirm all reservations, arrange currency, check-in online |
| **1 week out** | Final packing, copies of documents, notify bank |
| **Day before** | Charge devices, print essential docs, set OOO messages |

### Booking Priority Matrix

| Urgency | Item | Why |
|---------|------|-----|
| **Book Immediately** | Flights during peak season | Prices rise |
| **Book Immediately** | Popular attractions (Eiffel Tower, Vatican, etc.) | Sells out weeks ahead |
| **Book 1-2 Months Out** | Accommodations | Best selection |
| **Book 2-4 Weeks Out** | Popular restaurants | Reservations needed |
| **Book 1-2 Weeks Out** | Day trips, tours | Confirm availability |
| **Book Day Before/Of** | Local transport, minor activities | Flexibility |

## Currency and Payment

### Payment Method Strategy

| Method | Best Use | Watch Out For |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| **Credit card (no FX fee)** | Major purchases, hotels | Not accepted everywhere |
| **Debit card (no ATM fee)** | Cash withdrawals | Foreign ATM surcharges |
| **Local currency cash** | Small vendors, tips, transit | Carrying too much |
| **Home currency** | Minimal emergency backup | Poor exchange rates abroad |

### How Much Cash to Carry

| Destination Type | Cash vs. Card |
|------------------|---------------|
| Developed countries | 20% cash, 80% card |
| Developing countries | 50% cash, 50% card |
| Remote areas | 80% cash, 20% card |

**ATM Strategy**
- Withdraw larger amounts less frequently (fewer fees)
- Use bank ATMs, not standalone machines
- Always decline "dynamic currency conversion"
- Know daily withdrawal limits
