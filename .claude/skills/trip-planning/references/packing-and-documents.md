# Packing and Travel Documents Guide

Comprehensive packing lists and document requirements for travel preparation.

## Essential Documents

### Required for All International Travel

| Document | Requirements | Lead Time |
|----------|--------------|-----------|
| **Passport** | Valid 6+ months beyond return date | Renew 6-8 weeks ahead |
| **Visa** | Varies by destination and citizenship | Check 3+ months ahead |
| **Travel Insurance** | Covers medical, cancellation, belongings | Purchase with booking |
| **Flight Confirmations** | Print and digital copies | Confirm 24-48 hours ahead |
| **Accommodation Confirmations** | Address, contact, confirmation number | Have offline copies |

### Recommended Documents

| Document | Purpose | Notes |
|----------|---------|-------|
| Driver's license | Car rental, ID | International permit for some countries |
| Credit cards (2+) | Backup payment | From different banks |
| Travel insurance card | Emergency claims | Know policy number |
| Emergency contacts | Quick reference | Home + destination contacts |
| Medical info | Allergies, conditions | Translated if needed |
| Copies of prescriptions | Medication validation | Doctor's letter for controlled substances |

### Document Backup Strategy

**Physical Copies**
- Keep in separate location from originals
- Leave copy with trusted person at home
- Store in hotel safe when not needed

**Digital Copies**
- Email to yourself
- Cloud storage (Google Drive, Dropbox)
- Offline phone storage
- Dedicated travel app

## Visa Requirements Quick Reference

### Common Visa Categories

| Entry Type | Description | Examples |
|------------|-------------|----------|
| **Visa-free** | No visa required | US citizens to most of Europe |
| **Visa on arrival** | Get at airport | Many SE Asian countries |
| **eVisa** | Apply online before | Australia ETA, India eVisa |
| **Embassy visa** | Apply in advance | Russia, China, Brazil |

### Research Checklist
- [ ] Check visa requirements for your citizenship
- [ ] Note processing times (can be 2-8 weeks)
- [ ] Check for transit visa requirements
- [ ] Verify passport validity requirements
- [ ] Check COVID/health requirements (if still applicable)
- [ ] Look for visa fees and payment methods

## Climate-Specific Packing

### Tropical/Hot Weather

| Essential | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| Lightweight, breathable fabrics | Comfort in heat |
| Moisture-wicking underwear | Humidity management |
| Wide-brim hat | Sun protection |
| Sunglasses (polarized) | Glare, especially near water |
| Reef-safe sunscreen | Skin protection + environmental |
| Insect repellent | Mosquito protection |
| Light rain jacket | Afternoon tropical showers |
| Sandals with back strap | Beach + walking |
| Light cover-up | Sun protection, temples |

### Cold Weather

| Essential | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| Base layers (merino/synthetic) | Warmth without bulk |
| Insulated jacket | Core warmth |
| Waterproof outer layer | Rain/snow protection |
| Warm hat, gloves | Extremity protection |
| Scarf/neck gaiter | Versatile warmth |
| Warm socks (wool blend) | Foot comfort |
| Waterproof boots | Wet/snowy conditions |
| Hand/toe warmers | Extreme cold backup |

### Variable Weather

| Essential | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| Layers (easy to add/remove) | Temperature fluctuations |
| Packable rain jacket | Unexpected rain |
| Versatile shoes | Multiple conditions |
| Light scarf | Temperature transitions |
| Compact umbrella | City walking in rain |

## Packing Methods

### Roll vs. Fold

| Method | Best For | Benefits |
|--------|----------|----------|
| Rolling | T-shirts, casual clothes | Saves space, reduces wrinkles |
| Folding | Dress shirts, suits | Crisp appearance |
| Bundle wrapping | Mix of items | Minimizes wrinkles |
| Packing cubes | Organization | Easy access, compression |

### Packing Order (Bottom to Top)

1. Heavy items (shoes, toiletry bag)
2. Rolled/bulky items
3. Folded items
4. Delicates/easily wrinkled items
5. Items needed during transit (top or carry-on)

### Space-Saving Tips
- Wear bulkiest items on plane (jacket, boots)
- Use inside of shoes for socks/small items
- Compression bags for bulky items
- Multi-use items (sarong = towel, blanket, cover-up)
- Sample sizes or decant into travel bottles
- One outfit per day max (less for laundry trips)

## Luggage Guidelines

### Carry-On Size Limits

| Airline Type | Typical Dimensions | Weight |
|--------------|-------------------|--------|
| Full service | 22" x 14" x 9" | 15-25 lbs |
| Budget airline | 20" x 14" x 8" | 15-22 lbs |
| European budget | Often stricter | 7-10 kg |

*Always verify with specific airline before travel*

### Checked Bag Limits

| Standard | Dimensions | Weight |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| Economy | 62" total (L+W+H) | 50 lbs / 23 kg |
| Business/First | Same dimensions | 70 lbs / 32 kg |
| Overweight | Same | Pay surcharge |
| Oversized | Over 62" | Pay surcharge |

### Packing Quantity Guidelines

| Trip Length | Luggage Size | Check or Carry? |
|-------------|--------------|-----------------|
| Weekend (2-3 days) | Personal item + small carry-on | Carry-on only |
| 1 week | Carry-on suitcase | Carry-on preferred |
| 2 weeks | Medium checked bag | Check or pack light |
| 3+ weeks | Large checked bag | Check + laundry plan |
