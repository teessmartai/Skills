## Option A: Great Wolf Lodge Niagara (All-Inclusive Resort)

### Overview
- **Location:** Niagara Falls, ON (~1.5 hours from Toronto)
- **Type:** All-inclusive waterpark resort (must be hotel guest)
- **Best for:** Convenience, everything under one roof, rainy day backup built-in

### Waterpark Features (84°F Indoor)
- **Fort Mackenzie:** 4-story interactive treehouse with slides (perfect for ages 2-6)
- **Cub Pups:** Zero-depth entry splash area for toddlers
- **Slap Tail Pond:** Wave pool with gentle waves
- **Lazy River:** Family float experience
- **Wooly Mammoth:** Family raft ride
- **Niagara Rapids Run:** Water coaster (for the 6-year-old)

### Estimated Pricing (3 nights: June 9-12)

| Item | Cost (CAD) |
|------|------------|
| Wolf Den Suite (sleeps 6, kids bunk beds) x 3 nights | $1,200 - $1,500 |
| Early Saver Discount (25% off for 3 nights) | -$300 to -$375 |
| **Subtotal Room** | **$900 - $1,125** |
| Paw Pass x 5 (arcade, MagiQuest, activities) | $250 - $350 |
| Food (on-site restaurants, 3 days) | $400 - $500 |
| **Total Estimate** | **$1,550 - $1,975** |

### Pros
- Waterpark access from 1pm check-in day until 9pm checkout day
- No driving between hotel and waterpark
- Indoor park = no weather worries
- Kid-friendly suites with bunk beds
- Character appearances (Wiley the Wolf)
- MagiQuest interactive wand game

### Cons
- More expensive overall
- No day passes available
- On-site food is pricey
- Less variety (same waterpark all 3 days)

### Booking Tips
- Book at least 120 days ahead for Early Saver (25-30% off)
- Choose Tuesday or Wednesday arrival
- Ask about CAA/CARP discounts
- Book direct at greatwolf.com for best flexibility

## Option B: Fallsview Indoor Waterpark + Nearby Hotel

### Overview
- **Location:** Niagara Falls, ON (~1.5 hours from Toronto)
- **Type:** Separate waterpark with day passes OR hotel packages
- **Best for:** Budget flexibility, exploring Niagara Falls area

### Waterpark Features (125,000 sq ft)
- **Tiny-Tots Splash Park:** Dedicated toddler area with mini slides, splash pools, water tables
- **Wave Pool:** Full-sized wave pool
- **16 Waterslides:** Various heights for different ages
- **1,000-Gallon Tipping Bucket:** Kids love this!
- **Lazy River**
- **Outdoor Pool/Sundeck** (seasonal)

### Accommodation Options

#### Option B1: Skyline Hotel & Waterpark (Connected to Waterpark)
| Item | Cost (CAD) |
|------|------------|
| Family Suite x 2 nights | $280 - $400 |
| Waterpark Package (includes 4 passes x 2 days) | Included |
| Additional pass for 5th person x 2 days | $65 - $95 |
| Third night at budget hotel nearby | $100 - $150 |
| Food (mix of on-site and nearby restaurants) | $300 - $400 |
| **Total Estimate** | **$745 - $1,045** |

#### Option B2: Budget Hotel + Day Passes
| Item | Cost (CAD) |
|------|------------|
| Quality Inn/Comfort Inn x 3 nights | $300 - $450 |
| Waterpark Day Passes x 2 days (2.5 yr old FREE) | $280 - $380 |
| Food (restaurants, groceries) | $250 - $350 |
| Parking at waterpark ($35/day x 2) | $70 |
| **Total Estimate** | **$900 - $1,250** |

### Waterpark Pricing
| Age | Day Pass |
|-----|----------|
| Adult (13+) | ~$47 |
| Child (3-12) | ~$47 |
| Under 3 | FREE |

**Your family:** 2 adults + 2 kids (6 & 4) + 1 FREE (2.5) = 4 paid passes

### Pros
- Day passes available (flexibility)
- Dedicated toddler area
- Connected hotel option (Skyline)
- Access to all Niagara Falls attractions
- Generally lower total cost
- More dining options nearby

### Cons
- Need to manage waterpark hours (1pm arrival, 3pm departure next day)
- May need to drive/walk between hotel and park
- Weather could affect plans to explore area

## Option C: Canada's Wonderland + Splash Works (Day Trip Alternative)

### Overview
- **Location:** Vaughan, ON (~45 min from Toronto)
- **Type:** Theme park with included waterpark
- **Best for:** Budget option, shorter trip, amusement rides too

### Key Features
- **Splash Works:** 20-acre waterpark included with admission
- **KidZville:** Dedicated area for small children
- **Planet Snoopy:** Character meet & greets
- **17 Water Slides**

### Estimated Cost (2-day trip)
| Item | Cost (CAD) |
|------|------------|
| 2-Day Tickets x 4 (kids 3 & under may be free) | $400 - $500 |
| Nearby hotel x 1 night | $150 - $200 |
| Food | $150 - $200 |
| Parking ($30/day x 2) | $60 |
| **Total Estimate** | **$760 - $960** |

### Pros
- Lowest cost option
- Close to Toronto
- Rides + waterpark combo
- Snoopy character appearances

### Cons
- Outdoor only (weather dependent)
- Very crowded in June
- Less immersive waterpark experience
- May be overwhelming for 2.5-year-old

## Non-Waterpark Activities (Niagara Falls Area)

Plan 1-2 days of alternative activities to give kids a break from the water.

### Top Picks for Ages 2-6

| Activity | Cost (5 people) | Duration | Notes |
|----------|-----------------|----------|-------|
| **Bird Kingdom** | ~$85 | 1.5-2 hrs | World's largest indoor aviary, stroller friendly |
| **Butterfly Conservatory** | ~$90 | 1-1.5 hrs | 2,000+ butterflies, magical for kids |
| **Journey Behind the Falls** | ~$55 | 45 min | Kids 5 & under FREE, yellow ponchos! |
| **Niagara Falls viewing** | FREE | 1-2 hrs | Watch the falls, free splash pads |
| **Clifton Hill Fun Pass** | ~$120 | 3-4 hrs | SkyWheel, mini golf, arcades |
| **Happy Rolph's Animal Farm** | FREE | 1-2 hrs | Farm animals, playground (St. Catharines) |
| **Botanical Gardens** | ~$15 parking | 1-2 hrs | Free gardens, run around space |
| **Safari Niagara** | ~$140 | Half day | 1,000+ animals, petting areas |

### Activity Ideas by Day

**Rainy Day / Waterpark Break:**
- Bird Kingdom (indoor)
- Butterfly Conservatory (indoor)
- Clifton Hill arcades & attractions

**Active Day:**
- Morning: Butterfly Conservatory
- Lunch: Clifton Hill
- Afternoon: Journey Behind the Falls
- Evening: Watch illuminated falls

**Budget Day:**
- Morning: Botanical Gardens (free, parking $15)
- Afternoon: Happy Rolph's Animal Farm (free)
- Evening: Free splash pads in Niagara Falls parks
